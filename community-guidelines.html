Community Guidelines

Last updated: 29 July 2026
Chibwenzi only works if it feels safe. These rules apply to everyone. Breaking them can get your account removed without a refund. If you see something wrong, use Report in the app — every report is reviewed by a real person.

1. Be 18 or older

Chibwenzi is strictly for adults. Accounts believed to belong to anyone under 18 are removed immediately. If you suspect a member is underage, report them at once.

2. Be yourself

  • Use recent photos of yourself. No photos of other people, celebrities or stock images.
  • No pretending to be someone you are not, and no fake ages or names.
  • Verify your profile with a selfie — it protects everyone.

3. Never ask for money

Romance scams are the most common harm on dating apps. Never send money, airtime, gift cards or your Mobile Money PIN to anyone you meet here — no matter the story. Asking other members for money is banned and gets the account removed.

4. Treat people with respect

No harassment, threats, hate speech, insults or pressure of any kind. No unsolicited sexual messages or images. If someone says they are not interested, stop contacting them — and use Block, it is there for you.

5. Keep it decent

No nudity or sexually explicit photos, no violence or gore, no drugs or weapons, and nothing that breaks Zambian law. Photos must not contain other people’s faces without their consent, or any contact details.

6. No commercial use

Chibwenzi is for meeting people, not for advertising, selling, recruiting, prostitution or promoting other services.

7. Protect your privacy

We only show your first name, age and city — never your exact location or phone number. Please keep it that way: do not post your phone number, home or work address, bank or Mobile Money details in your profile or early chats.

8. Meeting in person

  • Meet in a public place, in daylight, the first few times.
  • Tell a friend or family member where you are going and when you expect to be back.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back.
  • If something feels wrong, leave. You never owe anyone an explanation.

9. Reporting and enforcement

Every report is reviewed. Depending on what we find, we may warn a member, remove content, suspend the account or permanently ban the person and their device. Serious matters — threats, fraud, or anything involving a minor — are removed on sight and may be referred to the Zambian authorities.

To report someone: open their profile or your chat with them and tap the shield icon → choose a reason. Or email office@orizisgroup.com.

10. If you feel unsafe

If you are in immediate danger, contact the Zambia Police on 991 (or 999). Then tell us at office@orizisgroup.com so we can act on the account.
