Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026
This policy explains what personal data Chibwenzi (operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED) collects and why. We process data in line with the Zambia Data Protection Act, No. 3 of 2021.

1. Who is responsible

ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED, registration no. 120241001416, TPIN 2002580846, of 41 Kasho Road, Roma Park, Lusaka, Lusaka Province, Zambia is the data controller. Contact: office@orizisgroup.com.

2. What we collect

  • Profile you provide: first name, age, gender, city, bio, interests and the photos you upload.
  • Verification selfie (optional): used only to confirm you are a real person. It is reviewed by our moderation team and is never shown on your public profile or to other members.
  • Account: an anonymous account identifier, and your email address only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Activity: your likes and passes, your matches, and the messages you exchange with matches.
  • Reports you submit or that are submitted about you, so we can keep the community safe.
  • Basic technical data (device/browser) if analytics are enabled.
What we deliberately do NOT collect: we do not track your GPS or precise location — only the city you choose yourself. We never show your phone number, email address or exact location to other members.

3. How we use it

  • To show your profile to other members and suggest matches (men are shown to women and women to men).
  • To let you chat with people you have matched with.
  • To verify accounts, review reports, and remove fake, underage or abusive profiles.
  • To process your subscription payment and support requests.
  • To prevent fraud and improve the service.

3a. Who can see what

  • Other members see: your first name, age, city, bio, interests and photos.
  • Only your matches see: the messages you send them.
  • Only our moderation team sees: your verification selfie and any reports. Private conversations between members are not routinely read by staff; they are reviewed only when reported.

4. Payment data

Mobile Money payments are processed by pawaPay and the mobile networks. We never see or store your Mobile Money PIN.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (payment, delivery, email), under confidentiality.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the service and legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Under the Zambia Data Protection Act, 2021 you may access, correct or delete your data. Email office@orizisgroup.com.

7a. Deleting your account

You can delete your Chibwenzi account and your data at any time, and you do not need to ask our permission:

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete my account and confirm. This is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • By email: write to office@orizisgroup.com telling us your first name and city, and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Deleted immediately: your profile, your photos, your verification selfie, your likes, your matches and the messages you sent.

What we may keep, and why: if an account was removed for abuse or fraud, we keep a minimal record (the report and a blocked-account identifier) for up to 24 months so a removed user cannot simply re-register. Records of a payment are kept for the period required by Zambian tax law. Nothing we keep is visible to other members.

8. Children

Chibwenzi is for adults aged 18 and over.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this policy. Questions: office@orizisgroup.com.
