Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026
This policy explains when you can get your money back for Chibwenzi Premium. It sits alongside your rights under the Competition and Consumer Protection Act, 2010, which it does not limit.

1. What you are buying

Chibwenzi Premium is a digital subscription that unlocks features inside the app for 30 days. It is not a physical product, so there is no delivery and no shipping.

During the launch offer (until 31 October 2026) Premium is free for everyone. No payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund.

2. It does not auto-renew

Your subscription simply ends after 30 days. There is no recurring charge and nothing to cancel. If you want another 30 days you buy it again.

3. Cooling-off — 48 hours

If you change your mind, contact us within 48 hours of payment and we will refund you in full, provided you have not made substantial use of the paid features (for example sending a large number of likes or messages). We apply this generously and in good faith.

4. When we always refund

  • You were charged but Premium was not activated.
  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • You were charged an amount different from the price shown.
  • A fault on our side made the paid features unusable for a substantial part of the period — we refund that part.
  • We closed your account for a reason that was not your fault — we refund the unused part.

5. When we do not refund

  • You used the paid period and then simply changed your mind after 48 hours.
  • You did not get matches, replies or a relationship. Premium unlocks features; it cannot promise you people.
  • Your account was removed for breaking the Community GuidelinesChild Safety — for example fraud, harassment, a fake profile or being under 18.
  • You gave money to another member. Never do this — we cannot recover it, and it is always a scam.

6. How to ask for a refund

Email office@orizisgroup.com with the Mobile Money number you paid from, the date, the amount and what happened. We reply within 5 business days and, where a refund is due, pay it to the same Mobile Money wallet within 14 days. Refunds are made in Zambian Kwacha. We do not charge a fee to process a refund; any charge levied by your mobile network is outside our control.

7. If you are not satisfied

Tell us first — we would rather fix it. You may also refer the matter to the Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (CCPC), or to ZICTA for electronic-transaction matters.
