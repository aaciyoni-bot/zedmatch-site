Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 2 August 2026
Chibwenzi is an adults-only dating service for people aged 18 and over. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). This page is our published standard, as required by the Google Play Child Safety Standards policy. It explains what we prohibit, how we prevent it, how anyone can report it, and who to contact.

1. Our standard: zero tolerance for CSAE

The following are absolutely prohibited on Chibwenzi, without exception and regardless of intent:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, real or generated.
  • Sexualisation of a minor in text, images, video or audio.
  • Grooming, solicitation, sextortion, or any attempt by an adult to make sexual contact with a minor.
  • Trafficking of a minor, or advertising a minor for any sexual purpose.
  • Use of the service by anyone under 18, whether or not they misstate their age.

Any account found to be involved in the above is removed permanently and immediately, with no refund, and the account and device are blocked from re-registering.

2. How we keep minors off Chibwenzi

  • 18+ only. An age gate appears before any profile can be created, and the app is rated and distributed as an adult application.
  • Minimum age enforced at sign-up. A date of birth under 18 cannot complete onboarding.
  • Selfie verification. Members can verify with a live selfie, which is reviewed by our moderation team. Verification helps us identify accounts that appear to belong to a minor.
  • Removal on suspicion. Where we believe an account belongs to a person under 18, we remove it. We do not require proof before acting.
  • No public content. Profiles and messages are visible only to matched adult members; nothing on Chibwenzi is publicly browsable.

3. How to report CSAE

Reporting is available to everyone, inside and outside the app, and does not require an account:

  • In the app: open the profile or the chat, tap the shield icon, and choose a reason. Reports involving a minor are prioritised.
  • By email, from anywhere: office@orizisgroup.com. Please include the member's first name and city, and what you saw.

Every report reaches a human moderator. Reports that involve a minor are escalated ahead of all other work and actioned as quickly as we are able.

4. What we do when CSAE is reported or detected

  • The content is removed and the account is suspended immediately, before any further review.
  • We preserve the relevant records so they can be provided to law enforcement.
  • Confirmed CSAE is reported to the Zambia Police Service and, where applicable, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • The account is permanently banned and blocked from re-registering.

5. Compliance with the law

Chibwenzi is operated by ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED, registered in Zambia. We comply with the child protection provisions of Zambian law, including the Children's Code Act No. 12 of 2022 and the Cyber Security and Cyber Crimes Act No. 2 of 2021, and with the data protection obligations of the Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021. Where a member is located outside Zambia, we also act in line with applicable local child safety law.

6. Designated point of contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters, including requests from law enforcement and from Google:

  • Email: office@orizisgroup.com
  • Operator: ORIZIS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED, Zambia
If a child is in immediate danger, contact the Zambia Police on 991 (or 999) first, then tell us so we can act on the account.

7. Review of this standard

We review this standard at least once a year, and after any incident, so that it continues to reflect how Chibwenzi actually operates.
